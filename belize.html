Belize

Belize is a small country in Central America that is particularly vulnerable to the impacts of climate change.

Rising sea levels, increased frequency of hurricanes, and changes in rainfall patterns pose significant threats to Belize's ecosystems, economy, and communities.

Efforts are being made to address these challenges through sustainable development initiatives, conservation programs, and climate adaptation strategies.
